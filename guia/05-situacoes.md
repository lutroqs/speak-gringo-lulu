**Cliente x Caixa**

> **Cashier:** Hi! Did you find everything okay?
> **You:** Yes, thanks.
> **Cashier:** Do you have a rewards card?
> **You:** No, I don't.
> **Cashier:** No problem. Do you need a bag?
> **You:** Yes, please. Can I pay by card?
> **Cashier:** Sure. Just tap or insert here. Your total is $34.90.
> **You:** Here you go.
> **Cashier:** Would you like your receipt?
> **You:** Yes, please. Thanks, have a good one!

*(Caixa: Oi! Encontrou tudo? / Você: Sim, obrigado. / Caixa: Tem cartão fidelidade? / Você: Não. / Caixa: Sem problema. Precisa de sacola? / Você: Sim. Posso pagar no cartão? / Caixa: Claro, é só aproximar ou inserir aqui. O total é 34,90. / Você: Aqui está. / Caixa: Quer a nota? / Você: Sim. Obrigado, bom dia!)*

**Cliente x Atendente**

> **You:** Excuse me, where can I find the peanut butter?
> **Employee:** It's in aisle 7, next to the jams.
> **You:** And do you have gluten-free bread?
> **Employee:** Yes, it's in the bakery section, on the left.
> **You:** Great, thank you!

*(Você: Com licença, onde fica a pasta de amendoim? / Funcionário: No corredor 7, ao lado das geleias. / Você: E vocês têm pão sem glúten? / Funcionário: Sim, na seção da padaria, à esquerda. / Você: Ótimo, obrigado!)*

**Cliente x Gerente**

> **You:** Hi, I'd like to speak to the manager, please.
> **Manager:** I'm the manager. How can I help you?
> **You:** I was charged twice for this item. Here's my receipt.
> **Manager:** I'm so sorry about that. Let me check… You're right. I'll refund the difference to your card.
> **You:** Thank you. How long does the refund take?
> **Manager:** Usually three to five business days.

*(Você: Oi, gostaria de falar com o gerente. / Gerente: Sou eu. Como posso ajudar? / Você: Fui cobrado duas vezes por este item. Aqui está a nota. / Gerente: Sinto muito. Deixa eu ver… Você tem razão. Vou estornar a diferença no seu cartão. / Você: Obrigado. Quanto tempo demora o estorno? / Gerente: Normalmente de três a cinco dias úteis.)*

> **Staff:** Sure. Where would you like to sit? We have seats in row F.
> **You:** Row F is perfect. Is it in English with subtitles?

> **You:** Okay. And a large popcorn and two medium Cokes.
> **Staff:** Butter on the popcorn?

> **Friend:** Right? But it was a bit too long for me.
> **You:** True, the middle part dragged a little. But the acting was amazing.

**Simulação completa: check-in e imigração**

> **Agent:** Good morning. Passport, please. Where are you flying today?
> **You:** To Toronto, with a connection in New York.
> **Agent:** Are you checking any bags?
> **You:** Yes, one bag. And can I have an aisle seat, please?
> **Agent:** Sure. Here's your boarding pass. Boarding starts at 9:40, gate 22.
>
> *(Na imigração)*
> **Officer:** What's the purpose of your visit?
> **You:** Tourism. I'm here for two weeks.
> **Officer:** Where are you staying?
> **You:** At a hotel downtown. Here's my reservation.
> **Officer:** Do you have a return ticket?
> **You:** Yes, I'm flying back on the 15th.
> **Officer:** Okay. Enjoy your stay.

### Hotel

> **You:** Hi, I have a reservation under the name Silva.
> **Receptionist:** Welcome! Can I see your ID and a credit card for incidentals?
> **You:** Sure. What time is check-out?
> **Receptionist:** Check-out is at 11 a.m. Breakfast is from 6:30 to 10.
> **You:** Is the Wi-Fi free?
> **Receptionist:** Yes, the password is on your key card holder.
> **You:** Great. Could I get a late check-out on my last day?
> **Receptionist:** I'll make a note. We'll confirm the day before.

### Táxi e Uber

> **You:** Hi, are you Mike? The Uber for Lucas?
> **Driver:** Yes, hop in! Going to the Hilton downtown, right?
> **You:** That's right. How long will it take?
> **Driver:** About 25 minutes with this traffic.
> **You:** Could you turn the AC up a little, please?
> **Driver:** Sure thing.

Frases úteis: *Can you drop me off here?* (Pode me deixar aqui?) · *Keep the change.* (Fique com o troco.) · *Can I pay by card?* (Aceita cartão?)

## Conversação {#conversacao}

**Apresentação pessoal**

> *Hi, I'm Luisa. I'm from São Paulo, Brazil. I work with data and finance, and I'm learning English because I want to work for an international company. In my free time, I like watching series and going to the gym.*

**Fazer amizades**

> **A:** Hey, I don't think we've met. I'm Sam.
> **You:** Hi Sam, I'm Luisa. Nice to meet you! How do you know Anna?
> **A:** We work together. What about you?
> **You:** We went to college together. So, what do you do for fun?
> **A:** I'm really into hiking. You?
> **You:** Same! We should go hiking sometime.

**Namoro**

> **A:** I had a really great time tonight.
> **You:** Me too. I'd love to see you again.
> **A:** How about dinner on Saturday?
> **You:** It's a date!

Expressões: *ask someone out* (chamar para sair) · *go on a date* (ir a um encontro) · *have a crush on* (ter uma queda por) · *break up* (terminar).

**Faculdade**

> **A:** What's your major?
> **You:** I'm majoring in Accounting, with a minor in Data Science.
> **A:** Nice! Which classes are you taking this semester?
> **You:** Corporate Finance and Statistics. The statistics professor is tough, though.

**Trabalho**

> **A:** How's the project going?
> **You:** Pretty well. We're a bit behind schedule, but we should finish by Friday.
> **A:** Do you need any help?
> **You:** Actually, yes. Could you review the dashboard before I send it to the client?

**Networking**

> **A:** So, what do you do?
> **You:** I'm a data analyst at a fintech in São Paulo. I build Power BI dashboards for the finance team.
> **A:** Interesting! I'm in fintech too.
> **You:** Oh, really? Would you mind if I connected with you on LinkedIn?
> **A:** Not at all. Here's my card.

### Perguntas de entrevista (com dica de resposta)

| Pergunta | Tradução | Como responder |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tell me about yourself. | Fale sobre você. | Presente → passado → futuro, em 1 a 2 minutos |
| Why do you want to work here? | Por que quer trabalhar aqui? | Cite algo específico da empresa |
| What are your strengths? | Quais seus pontos fortes? | 2 pontos fortes, cada um com exemplo |
| What is your biggest weakness? | Qual sua maior fraqueza? | Algo real + o que você faz para melhorar |
| Tell me about a time you solved a problem. | Conte uma vez em que resolveu um problema. | Método STAR: Situation, Task, Action, Result |
| Tell me about a conflict with a coworker. | Conte um conflito com um colega. | STAR, com foco em comunicação |
| Where do you see yourself in five years? | Onde se vê em 5 anos? | Crescimento ligado à vaga |
| How do you handle tight deadlines? | Como lida com prazos apertados? | Priorização + exemplo |
| Walk me through a dashboard you built. | Explique um dashboard que você criou. | Objetivo → dados → visuais → impacto |
| How would you explain a technical result to a non-technical audience? | Como explicaria um resultado técnico para leigos? | Comece pelo impacto no negócio |
| What is the difference between a measure and a calculated column in Power BI? | Diferença entre medida e coluna calculada? | Medida: calculada na consulta; coluna: armazenada no modelo |
| How do you ensure data quality? | Como garante a qualidade dos dados? | Validação, testes, reconciliação |
| Why are you leaving your current job? | Por que está saindo do emprego atual? | Sempre positivo: crescimento |
| What are your salary expectations? | Qual sua pretensão salarial? | Faixa pesquisada + abertura para negociar |
| Do you have any questions for us? | Tem alguma pergunta? | Sempre tenha 2 ou 3 perguntas prontas |
